# Overnight decisions

One line per decision made while Geoffrey was away: request number, what was chosen, the main alternative, and where to change it.

| Req | Decision | Alternative | Where to change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Two tabs above the categories: "Notes, reading & markings" and "Rhythms". Only the open tab is played; picks in the other tab are kept but ignored. | A separate "mode" switch, or disabling the other categories in place | `TABS` in js/content.js; `tabSubs`/`activeSubs` in js/game.js |
| 1 | A subcategory's checkbox now switches it on/off: on restores its last picks (or its defaults), off clears them but remembers them. A small "All" button picks every chip. | Checkbox = select all / clear (old behavior) | `.selall` change handler and `defaultChips` in js/game.js; `defaults` on a subcategory in js/content.js |
| 1 | Final barline moved to Repeats along with repeat sign, endings, D.C. al Fine, Fine, Coda; accidentals stay in Bowing & articulation. | Leave final barline in Bowing & articulation | `REPEAT_ITEMS` / `RELOCATED_CHIPS` in js/content.js |
| 1 | Old saved settings that had both rhythms and other cards on open on the "Notes, reading & markings" tab. | Open on Rhythms | `loadSettings` in js/game.js |
| 2 | The instrument switch sits in the page header. Violin = treble clef, Viola = alto clef, Both = a "Clef for notes" choice: Alternate (default), Treble or Alto. Alternate means card 1 treble, card 2 alto, and so on, even when a pattern repeats a card. | Default Both to treble only; alternate per round instead of per card | `INSTRUMENTS`, `CLEF_MODES`, `clefFor` in js/content.js |
| 2 | Key signature cards also follow the clef (they show a clef even though they have no notes). Clef cards and other Markings never change. | Keep key signature cards in treble always | `KEY_ITEMS` in js/content.js (`draw` vs `svg`) |
| 2 | Viola home position on the A string is A B C♯ D (like the violin's A string); only the violin's top string (E) gets the 4th-finger B. | Add E5 (4th finger) to the viola's A string | `HOME_SEMIS` in js/content.js |
| 2 | When you switch instrument and every string of the old one was picked in Home position, every string of the new one gets picked. | Leave the picks alone (C string would start off) | `instrumentSwitch` handler in js/game.js |
| 2 | Scale degree cards keep starting at or above G3 for every instrument. | Start at the viola's C3 in viola mode | `degreePool` in js/content.js |
| 3 | Chords is its own category (Naming chords, Spelling chords, Cadences), each with chips grouped Simple / Medium / Advanced / Uncommon; Simple is the default. | Put chords under Note reading | `NAMING_CHIPS`, `SPELL_CHIPS`, `CADENCE_CHIPS` and `TIERS` in js/content.js |
| 3 | Uncommon tier = Neapolitan 6th, V/V (and V7/V), other secondary dominants (V7/IV, V7/ii, V7/vi, V7/iii; in minor V7/iv, V7/VI, V7/III), borrowed chords (major: iv, ♭VI, ♭VII, ♭III; minor: Picardy I, VII, IV) and V13. | Other picks (e.g. Neapolitan in Advanced) | `SPELL_CHIPS` in js/content.js, `kindChords` in js/theory.js |
| 3 | Naming chords: roots are C C♯ D♭ D E♭ E F F♯ G A♭ A B♭ B; any chord that would need a double sharp or flat is left out. Answers are said like "D minor", "G7", "C major 7", "B half-diminished 7". Inversion chips (Medium) add 1st/2nd-inversion triads; the answer is still just the chord name, with the inversion in the small print. | Include the inversion in the answer ("C major, 2nd inversion") | `NAMING_ROOTS`, `namingPool` in js/content.js; `QUALITIES[].sym` in js/theory.js |
| 3 | Spelling answers go from the bass up: inversions (I 6/4, N6) and augmented sixths (A♭ C E♭ F♯) start with the bass note; everything else is root position. | Always spell from the root | `bassUp` in js/theory.js |
| 3 | Minor keys use natural minor for diatonic chords, except V, V7, vii° and vii°7, which use the raised leading tone; III+ (augmented) is in Advanced for minor keys, V+ for major keys. | Use harmonic minor throughout | `kindChords` in js/theory.js |
| 3 | Cadences are chips by cadence type: Simple = PAC, IAC, half (I–V, IV–V), plagal; Medium = deceptive, half from ii6, half from the cadential 6/4; Advanced = Phrygian half, Picardy third (minor keys only); Uncommon = minor plagal and deceptive to ♭VI (major keys only). Cards show two whole-note chords on one staff with the key signature, bass plus three close upper voices, and no Roman numerals. | Grand staff, or chord-tier chips like the other two subcategories | `CADENCES` in js/theory.js; `CADENCE_CHIPS`, `voiceChord`, `cadencePool` in js/content.js |
| 3 | Spelling chords and Cadences each have their own key picker (all 13 major and 13 minor keys, default C major) and share one "Roman numerals / Function names" switch; function names replace the numeral on spelling cards and in cadence answers. | One key picker for the whole category | `keyPicker`, `options`, `CHORD_LABELS` in js/content.js |
| 3 | Chord cards follow the instrument clef (treble / alto / alternate) like note cards. | Always treble | `draw` in `namingPool` / `cadencePool` |
| 4 | Time signatures in two groups: Simple = 2/4 3/4 4/4 C ¢ 3/8 6/8 (cut time sits next to C, as asked, even though 2/2 is in Complex); Complex = 2/2 3/2 5/4 7/8 9/8 12/8. Defaults when turned on: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4. | Put ¢ in Complex next to 2/2 | `METER_GROUPS`, `DEFAULT_METERS` in js/content.js |
| 4 | "Duplets in triple meters" read as compound meters (6/8, 9/8, 12/8, beat split in three); "triplets in duple meters" read as simple meters counted in beats (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, C, ¢, 2/2, 3/2, 5/4). 3/8 and 7/8 get neither. | Triplets in 2/4 and 4/4 only, duplets in 3/4 too | `cellFits` in js/content.js |
| 4 | The basic/advanced menu is replaced by a "Note values" row of symbol buttons (always on, at least one). Defaults: eighths, quarters, halves, whole notes, dotted halves, and rests. Rests are a button too and stay on by default, as the old Basic level had rests. Old "Advanced" settings turn on sixteenths, dotted eighths, dotted quarters, triplets and duplets as well. | Rests off by default, or always on with no button | `RHYTHM_VALUES`, `DEFAULT_RHYTHM_VALUES` in js/content.js; `loadSettings` in js/game.js |
| 4 | A dotted figure doesn't need its partner's button: "dotted eighth" brings its sixteenth, "dotted quarter" its eighth, "dotted half" (in 2/2) its quarter. | Require both buttons | `uses` lists in `BEAT_CELLS` / `MULTI_CELLS` |
| 4 | If the picked values can't fill a beat (e.g. only whole notes in 3/4), that beat gets one note the length of the beat. | Refuse to start, or show a warning | `makeMeasure` in js/content.js |
| 4 | Counting now shows a held beat when a later note falls in the same beat ("1 & 2 – &"). | Leave it out ("1 & 2 &") | `countTokens` in js/content.js |
| 5 | Intervals (in Note reading): chips P1 m2 M2 m3 M3 P4 A4 d5 P5 m6 M6 m7 M7 P8 m9 M9 m10 M10; off until turned on, then all are picked. Both notes come from the picked key's scale (natural minor for minor keys), shown with its key signature, so every interval is diatonic. Key picker default C major. | Lower note from the key, upper note any spelling (accidentals) | `intervalPool` in js/content.js, `INTERVALS` / `intervalsInKey` in js/theory.js |
| 5 | Intervals can be shown one after the other (default) or together (harmonic); answers are full names ("Major 3rd", "Perfect octave"). | Harmonic default; abbreviations (M3) | `INTERVAL_STYLES` in js/content.js |
| 5 | Scale degrees: the chips are now degrees 1-7 (all picked when turned on) and keys moved to a key picker (default C major, minor keys allowed; minor uses natural minor, so 7 is the subtonic). Saved key picks carry over. | Keep major keys only | `degreePool`, `DEGREE_IDS` in js/content.js; migration in `loadSettings` |
| 6 | One range control for all three diatonic modes: presets 1 / 2 / 3 octaves (default 1) and Custom, plus a two-ended slider over the natural notes from the instrument's lowest open string to B7. Presets start at the lowest tonic (Scale) or the lowest open string (Chromatic, Pick notes); moving a slider end switches to Custom. | Separate range controls per mode; sharps/flats as slider stops | `RANGE_PRESETS`, `rangeFor` in js/content.js; `#rangeSub` in index.html |
| 6 | "Class says: Note names / Fingerings" is one shared setting shown in both Home position and Diatonic note names. Fingerings are first position on the highest string that has the note ("Open D", "1 on D", "Low 2 on A", "High 3 on G"); above that, on the top string, the lowest odd position that reaches it ("3 in 3rd position on E"). Open strings mention the 4th-finger alternative in the small print. | Separate settings per subcategory; allow even positions; 4th finger instead of open strings | `fingering`, `FINGER_SAY`, `NOTE_ANSWERS` in js/content.js |
| 6 | In a mixed class, treble-clef cards get violin fingerings and alto-clef cards get viola fingerings (notes below the violin's G always use viola). | Show both fingerings on every card | `fingerInst` in js/content.js |
| 6 | Very high notes get high positions literally (e.g. viola G6 = "3 in 11th position on A"); nothing is capped. | Cap at 7th position and say "very high" | `fingering` in js/content.js |
